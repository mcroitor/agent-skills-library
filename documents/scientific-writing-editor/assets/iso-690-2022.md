# ISO 690:2022 Quick Reference

This guide supports citation and bibliography normalization for the Scientific Writing Editor skill.

## Scope and Policy
- Default citation/bibliography standard: ISO 690:2022.
- Do not mix styles within one document.
- If a venue requires another style, follow the venue style and mark the override explicitly.

## Citation Systems
Use one system consistently across the full document:
- Author-date system.
- Numeric system.

When the source input does not specify a system, prefer author-date unless the task explicitly requests numeric references.

## Core Bibliography Rules
- Keep author names and source titles exactly as in the source metadata.
- Preserve publication year, edition, publisher, and place when available.
- Include DOI when available.
- For online resources, include URL and access date when required by context.
- Keep punctuation and ordering consistent for all entries.

## Templates (Generic)
Use these templates as normalization targets. Adapt punctuation consistently within the chosen citation system.

### Book
- Author, A. Title. Edition. Place: Publisher, Year. ISBN.

### Journal Article
- Author, A. Article title. Journal title, Year, Volume(Issue), pages. DOI.

### Chapter in Edited Book
- Author, A. Chapter title. In: Editor, E. (ed.). Book title. Place: Publisher, Year, pages. ISBN.

### Conference Paper
- Author, A. Paper title. In: Conference title, place, date. Place: Publisher, Year, pages. DOI/URL.

### Thesis/Dissertation
- Author, A. Title. Thesis type. Institution, Place, Year.

### Web Resource
- Author/Organization. Title [online]. Year or date. Available from: URL [Accessed DD Mon YYYY].

## In-Text Citation Guidance
- Author-date: keep author spelling and year consistent with bibliography.
- Numeric: keep numbering stable and ordered by first appearance.
- Every in-text citation must map to one bibliography entry.
- Every bibliography entry should be cited in the text unless explicitly allowed otherwise.

## Validation Checklist
- Exactly one citation system is used.
- All citations have matching bibliography entries.
- No uncited bibliography items (unless intentionally included and marked).
- Required fields are present for each source type.
- DOI/URL/access-date handling is consistent.
- Duplicates are merged; near-duplicates are reconciled.

## Common Errors
- Mixing numeric and author-date citations.
- Missing year or publisher in book references.
- Missing issue/pages in journal references when available.
- DOI omitted for sources that have one.
- Inconsistent date formats in access dates.
