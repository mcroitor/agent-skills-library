## Transferability & Generalization Notes

**For Case Studies:** Emphasize that case studies don't generalize statistically. Instead, readers assess **transferability**:

- [ ] Context thoroughly described? (Readers can assess similarity to their own context)
- [ ] Lessons explicitly stated? (What can practitioners take away?)
- [ ] Theoretical insights articulated? (What broader principles emerge?)
