### Step 2: Convert to percentage

$$\text{Score \%} = \frac{\text{Weighted Sum}}{4.0} \times 100\% = \text{_______%}$$

### Step 3: Check critical criteria

- [ ] C23 (Plagiarism) scored 0–1? → **Auto-Reject**
