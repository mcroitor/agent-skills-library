## Step 3: Calculate Weighted Contribution

For each criterion with Weight > 0%:

$$\text{Weighted Contribution} = \text{Criterion Score} \times \frac{\text{Weight \%}}{100}$$

Example: If C7 = 2 and Weight = 15%, then Contribution = 2 × 0.15 = 0.30
