# Critical Criteria Desk Review Checklist

Use this checklist for **desk review (5–10 minutes)** — rapid evaluation of critical stopping criteria before sending to full peer review.

---

## ⚠ ABSOLUTE REJECTION TRIGGERS (Non-Negotiable)

If **any** of these score 0–1, **REJECT** immediately. Do not proceed to full review.

### [✓] Criterion 22: Compliance with Research Ethics

**Evidence required:** Look for:
- IRB/Ethics approval statement (or declaration why not needed)
- Informed consent documentation
- Data privacy & confidentiality measures
- Disclosure of conflicts of interest

**REJECT if:**
- No ethics approval mentioned for human/animal studies
- Evidence of fabricated data or unethical conduct
- Serious COI violations not disclosed

**Status:** [ ] Pass [ ] **REJECT**

**Evidence location (if reject):** _________________________________

---

### [✓] Criterion 23: Absence of Plagiarism

**Evidence required:** Look for:
- Paraphrasing with citations
- Quotation marks for direct text
- Proper attribution of figures/tables
- Self-plagiarism disclosure (if applicable)

**REJECT if:**
- Extensive verbatim copying without attribution
- Suspected self-plagiarism without declaration
- Manipulated figures/data

**Status:** [ ] Pass [ ] **REJECT**

**Evidence location (if reject):** _________________________________

## ⚠ SCIENTIFIC FATAL FLAWS (Type-Dependent Critical)

If **any** of these score 0–1 **for the article type**, recommend **Major Revision or Reject**. These can be fixed; absolute rejections cannot.

### [✓] Criterion 7: Methodological Correctness

**Applies to:** Empirical, Systematic Review, Case Study

**CRITICAL if:**
- [ ] Empirical: Methods not clearly described; no reproducibility
- [ ] Systematic Review: Search strategy missing; selection bias evident
- [ ] Case Study: Data collection process unclear

**Status:** [ ] Pass [ ] **Major Flaw**

**Evidence location:** _________________________________

---

### [✓] Criterion 9: Result Reliability & Validity

**Applies to:** Empirical, Systematic Review, Case Study

**CRITICAL if:**
- [ ] Results unsupported by data (e.g., no statistical p-values, confidence intervals)
- [ ] Sample size too small; power analysis missing
- [ ] Confounding factors not controlled

**Status:** [ ] Pass [ ] **Major Flaw**

**Evidence location:** _________________________________

---

### [✓] Criterion 2: Scientific Novelty (Type-Dependent)

**⚠ MARK N/A for:** Systematic Reviews, Replications, Datasets, Reviews of Reviews

**CRITICAL if (for applicable types):**
- [ ] Empirical: No new finding or hypothesis; purely replicates existing work
- [ ] Theoretical: Merely summarizes existing theory; no conceptual advance
- [ ] Case Study: Generic case with no distinguishing feature

**Status:** [ ] Pass [ ] N/A [ ] **Major Flaw**

**Evidence location:** _________________________________

## ⚠ EDITORIAL FILTER (Quality vs. Scope)

### [✓] Criterion 25: Alignment with Journal Scope

**Evidence required:** 
- Abstract clearly describes research question & findings
- Does research fit journal's stated scope?

**RECOMMENDATION (not scientific rejection):**
- [ ] Desk Reject to Editor (out of scope)
- [ ] Proceed to review

---

## DESK REVIEW DECISION

Based on critical criteria above:

- [ ] **All critical criteria PASS** → Send to full peer review
- [ ] **One or more fatal flaws (C7, C9, C2)** → Recommend **Major Revision** or **Desk Reject**
- [ ] **Ethics/Plagiarism fails (C22, C23)** → **REJECT immediately**
- [ ] **Out of scope (C25)** → **Desk Reject to Editor**
